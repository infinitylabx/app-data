This policy explains what Spinlume (package com.mika.spinart, the "App") by [PUBLISHER_NAME] ("we", "us") collects and why.

1. What stays on your device

The App has no account or sign-in. Your artworks, drawing drafts, records, settings and unlocked items are stored locally on your device. We do not receive your artworks. When you save a picture, it is written to your device's Pictures/Spinlume folder (using MediaStore on Android 10 and later; on Android 9 and earlier the storage write permission is used for this only).

3. Permissions the App uses

  • INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: load ads, remote content and settings; detect whether you are online.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): declared for push notifications; notifications are shown only if you allow it.
  • VIBRATE: haptic feedback when drawing and spinning.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 9 and earlier only): save your artwork pictures to the gallery.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID and Android advertising-services permissions declared by the ads SDKs): ads, attribution and fraud prevention.
  • Install referrer service and Google Play Billing (BILLING): tell Adjust and Meta where an install came from, and handle Premium purchases.
  • Network and background permissions added by SDKs (ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, push receive): detect network type for ads and receive push messages.

4. Data sharing

We share the data above only with the service providers listed (including all eight mediated ad networks and Adjust and Meta for measurement), under their own privacy policies: Google (AdMob, Firebase, Play), AppLovin, Meta, Adjust, Liftoff (Vungle), Mintegral, Pangle, InMobi, Moloco, Unity. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

5. Retention and your choices

Local data is removed when you delete artworks or uninstall the App. Analytics and advertising data is kept by the providers according to their policies. You can reset or opt out of the advertising ID in Android settings, deny the notification permission, and request deletion by emailing us: we delete Firebase data linked to your app instance ID and send a deletion request to Adjust. Uninstalling the App removes all artworks and local data.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.
