Spinlume is a neon drawing wheel. Draw on it while it turns, then swipe to spin your artwork in either direction and chase a new RPM record. Free to start, works offline.

See how it works

Draw and spin on the same canvas

Draw while it turns

The wheel spins by itself while you draw. Hold your finger still and you get a perfect circle. Pick from 16 glowing colors, mirror and symmetry modes.

Flick it like a real spinner

Swipe as many times as you like, in both directions, and touch to brake. Watch the RPM counter and beat your record.

Keep and share your art

Your artworks stay on your phone. Save a picture to your gallery or share it with friends whenever you like.
